I am **Ronit Roy Choudhary**, a computational biology researcher focused on integrating transcriptomics, chromatin interaction data, and machine learning to answer clinically relevant questions.

My current work centers on:

1. Machine learning models for Hi-C contact map prediction from RNA-seq.
2. Multi-omics integration for cancer transcriptomics and disease subtyping.
3. Biomarker discovery and survival modeling workflows.

I enjoy building reproducible end-to-end pipelines that combine strong statistical practice with practical biological interpretation.

## Collaboration Interests
